How the Risk Score Works

40%
Rainfall (48-hour)
The most critical short-term trigger. Higher recent rainfall dramatically increases the probability of surface flooding and run-off.
30%
Elevation
Lower-lying ground is more vulnerable to surface water and runoff. Areas around 15m or lower are especially at risk during heavy rain.
20%
Drainage Quality
Poor drainage — common in peri-urban and informal settlements — traps water and multiplies flood damage even at moderate rainfall levels.
10%
Soil Type
Clay soils absorb very little water, increasing run-off. Sandy soils absorb significantly more, reducing surface flood risk.
